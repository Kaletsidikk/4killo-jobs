## The Problem

Historically, **4 Kilo** was one of the central places in Addis Ababa where job seekers gathered around newsstands and bulletin boards to find employment opportunities.

Today, job discovery has moved online, but the problem of fragmentation remains.

In Ethiopia, opportunities are distributed across:
* 30+ Telegram channels
* Company career pages
* Public job boards
* NGO and organizational portals
* Other public online sources

The same vacancy often appears dozens of times simultaneously, while important details get buried inside chaotic posts, redirected through intermediary bots, or drowned in spam and promotional noise.

For job seekers, finding the **right** opportunity is often harder than finding *a* job post.

---

## Our Solution

**AratKilo** is a personalized job intelligence platform that aggregates job opportunities from multiple public sources, cleans and structures them, eliminates duplicate broadcast storms, extracts direct application information, and matches opportunities to individual job seekers.

Instead of forcing users to sift through dozens of channels, AratKilo creates a clean, unified discovery experience.

### Key Capabilities
* **Omni-Source Aggregation:** Pulls vacancies across Telegram channels and public career sites in real time.
* **Canonical Deduplication:** Merges identical postings into one verified, noise-free card.
* **Direct Hop Bypass:** Short-circuits intermediary bot loops to surface the real application link or contact.
* **Voice-First Search:** Search conversationally using natural voice in Amharic (አማርኛ), Afaan Oromoo, and English (powered by Voxide).
* **Personalized Matching:** Tailored alerts pushed directly based on individual skills, experience, and location.
* **Career Intelligence:** Research-grounded job assistance and market insights.
